# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@getresumehq.com** with "SECURITY" in the subject. Please include
what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.

Expect an acknowledgement within 5 days. Please give a reasonable window for a
fix before disclosing publicly.

## Scope

In scope:

- This repository
- The hosted app at https://getresumehq.com
- The scoring API at https://resume-scorer.fly.dev

Out of scope: findings that require an already-compromised device or account,
social engineering, denial of service, and reports produced solely by an
automated scanner with no demonstrated impact.

## Handling of personal data

The hosted service stores account emails and any resume text a user chooses to
save. If a report involves access to other users' data, say so explicitly and
do not download, retain, or share that data.
